# Understanding Jenkins Pipelines

Jenkins Pipelines are a suite of plugins which supports implementing and integrating continuous delivery pipelines into Jenkins.

### Pipeline as Code
By defining our pipeline in a `Jenkinsfile` committed to Git, we ensure that our CI/CD workflow is versioned, peer-reviewed, and easily reproducible.
